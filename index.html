01

Company thesis

Every fifty to one hundred years, a new infrastructure cycle replaces the last one. The group that builds the new rails owns the next era. Steel. Electricity. Oil. Semiconductors. The internet. AI and physical intelligence now form the next operating layer.

Africa cannot afford to enter this cycle as a passive consumer of someone else's stack. The continent holds the minerals, energy, young population, mobility demand, institutional whitespace and sovereign urgency that the next era requires. The strategic question is whether these layers remain fragmented or are coordinated into an operating system.

Silla Global was built to hold that position. We design, deploy and capitalise the full-stack architecture across intelligence, bits, electrons, atoms, capital and the institutions that hold them together.

The thesis is simple. The future will not be won by applications alone. It will be won by the frontier systems that connect compute, energy, data, policy, finance, infrastructure and human alignment into compounding deployment loops.

Who should apply

This application is for founders, governments, family offices, institutional capital allocators, infrastructure operators, AI teams, energy developers, media platforms and strategic organisations building at the frontier of Africa's transition.

Strong fit

You may be a strong fit if your mandate connects to one or more frontier layers.

  • AI, data, automation or sovereign intelligence systems.
  • Energy, mobility, charging, minerals or physical infrastructure.
  • Institutional capital, project finance, fund architecture or strategic LP relationships.
  • Government, regional economic development, public-private deployment or policy execution.
  • Media, narrative, audience infrastructure or AI-native growth systems.
Poor fit

We are unlikely to engage if the work is tactical only.

  • No clear decision-maker or mandate owner.
  • No implementation capacity or capital path.
  • Short-term campaign thinking without strategic compounding potential.

  • Misalignment with Africa's long-duration infrastructure and intelligence transition.
